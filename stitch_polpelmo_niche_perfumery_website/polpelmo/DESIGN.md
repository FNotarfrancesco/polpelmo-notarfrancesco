## Brand & Style

Polpelmo embodies bespoke Italian perfumery through an architectural lens. Rooted in *sprezzatura*—the art of effortless nonchalance—the visual language balances timeless Mediterranean warmth with rigorous, gallery-grade minimalism. It draws direct inspiration from raw travertine colonnades, unpolished Tuscan cypress wood, and artisanal flacons.

The target audience consists of discerning collectors, connoisseurs of rare olfactory compositions, and patrons who appreciate high-craft tactile objects over loud branding. The aesthetic sits at the intersection of high editorial publishing, severe Milanese rationalism, and tactile luxury:
- **Quiet Power:** Ample, intentional negative space creates an atmosphere of contemplation and prestige.
- **Material Authenticity:** Surfaces evoke tactile substrates—deckle-edge heavy paper stock, honed stone, aged bronze, and ink.
- **Architectural Rigor:** Strict vertical alignments and hairline demarcations establish rhythm without visual clutter.

## Colors

The palette references classic Italian materials: honed travertine, aged botanical oils, deep Tuscan cypress needles, and printer's ink.

- **Primary (`#16251E` — Tuscan Cypress):** A deep, saturated evergreen black. Used for definitive typography, authoritative framing, dominant actions, and anchor elements.
- **Secondary (`#C49A6C` — Warm Bronze):** Muted, reflective metallic tone used for accents, refined badges, focal highlights, and active indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#9F7542` — Deep Amber):** A rich, resinous undertone for secondary actions, interactive hovers, and warmth in metadata labels.
- **Neutral Base (`#F8F6F0` — Travertine):** Warm stone background serving as the default canvas. Evokes fine artisanal cotton paper.
- **Surface Elevation (`#F3EFE6` — Honed Alabaster):** Subtle tonal shift used for panels, containers, and card foundations.
- **Ink Black (`#111111` — Chiaro-Scuro):** Pure structural contrast reserved for editorial headlines, high-priority copy, and crisp iconography.
- **Hairline Neutral (`#E5DFD3` / `#22332A`):** Low-contrast borders that guide the eye without adding structural weight.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an interplay between Bodoni Moda’s dramatic, high-fashion contrast and Plus Jakarta Sans’s airy, disciplined precision.

- **Bodoni Moda:** Used exclusively for editorial moments, fragrance names, collection narratives, and hero declarations. Display and headline levels should lean into light-to-medium weights, allowing the extreme vertical strokes and hairline serifs to convey timeless sophistication.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Deployed for long-form olfactive breakdowns, technical extraction details, UI controls, and utility copy. Rendered in light (`300`) and regular (`400`) weights with deliberate, generous line-heights to promote breathing room.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Formatted in uppercase with extended tracking (`0.14em` to `0.18em`), echoing vintage flacon apothecary labels, laboratory batch plates, and gallery catalog notations.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a 12-column architectural grid with generous gutters and expansive margins that preserve the white space characteristic of an art monograph.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout with `4rem` outer page margins and `2rem` column gutters. Components never crowd the viewport edges. Asymmetrical arrangements—such as an 8-column hero image offset against a 4-column notes breakdown—create editorial rhythm.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout with `2.5rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Content blocks collapse thoughtfully, prioritizing dual-column product arrangements.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters. Stacks vertically while retaining generous vertical padding (`3rem` to `4rem` between major sections) to avoid a cramped transactional feel.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows deliberate increments of empty space. Margins framing products and editorial copy prioritize generous whitespace over compact data density.

## Elevation & Depth

This system discards heavy dropshadows and glossy visual gimmicks in favor of textural layering, hairline architectural framing, and subtle atmospheric shifts.

- **Tonal Stepping:** Elevation is communicated through subtle surface color shifts. The canvas sits at `#F8F6F0` (Travertine). Layered panels, drawers, and modal sheets elevate to `#F3EFE6` (Honed Alabaster) or deep Tuscan Cypress (`#16251E`) for immersive evening contexts.
- **Hairline Boundaries:** Depth and separation rely primarily on 1px crisp borders (`#E5DFD3` on light surfaces; `#23352B` on dark surfaces). This mimics fine bespoke stationery and brass framing.
- **Diffused Micro-Ambient Shading:** When an interactive layer floats over content (e.g., cart drawer, olfactory pyramid menu), use an ultra-diffused shadow tinted with Tuscan cypress: `0 20px 48px -12px rgba(22, 37, 30, 0.08)`. Shadows must never appear gray or muddy.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture relies on sharp lines, celebrating the unyielding corners of hand-cut stone blocks, heavy glass perfume flacons, and bespoke packaging boxes.

- **Sharp Edges (`roundedness: 0`):** All containers, buttons, imagery modules, modals, and input fields feature strict 90-degree right angles (`0px` border radius).
- **Architectural Integrity:** Form implies permanence and weight. Rounding is permitted only when referencing physical round forms (e.g., circular flacon cap icons, audio playback dials, or circular wax seals).
- **Proportional Frames:** Card containers maintain classical golden ratio proportions or strict 1:1 and 4:5 aspect ratios for photography.

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary:** Solid `#16251E` (Tuscan Cypress) background with `#F8F6F0` typography. Strict `0px` radius, zero drop shadow, 1px border matching the background. Padding: `1rem 2.25rem`. Text styled in uppercase `label-md` with extended letter-spacing. On hover: subtle color shift to `#20382C` with smooth 300ms ease.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Transparent background with a 1px border in `#16251E` or `#C49A6C`. Text matches the border color. On hover: inverts background and text with an effortless, calm transition.
- **Tertiary / Link:** Pure typographic control with a 1px solid underline spaced 4px below text. Underline expands outward from center on hover.

### Input Fields & Selects
- Framed by a 1px hairline border in `#E5DFD3` on a `#F8F6F0` background.
- Typography is set in `body-md`. Labels sit outside in tracked uppercase `label-sm`.
- Focus state: hairline shifts smoothly from `#E5DFD3` to `#16251E` without thick rings or glowing outlines.

### Cards & Presentation Modules
- Base cards use `#F3EFE6` or `#F8F6F0` bounded by a 1px hairline rule. No elevation shadows.
- Imagery inside cards spans edge-to-edge at the top, framed by an internal 1px border separator before olfactory metadata.
- Product listings highlight extraction methods, batch numbers, and key notes (Top, Heart, Base) arranged in clear horizontal typographic tiers.

### Lists & Olfactory Accords
- Specimen lists feature continuous horizontal hairline borders between line items.
- Item format: Title in `headline-sm` (Bodoni Moda) aligned left; ingredients, volume, and pricing set in `label-md` (Plus Jakarta Sans) aligned right.
- Row hover triggers a subtle background transition to `#F1ECE2`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Checkboxes: 16px × 16px square, 0px radius, 1px border in `#16251E`. Checked state fills with `#16251E` with an inset Travertine checkmark.
- Radio buttons: 16px × 16px sharp diamond or square indicator with an inner solid marker, deliberately diverging from generic rounded UI conventions.

### Bespoke Domain Components: Olfactory Notes Pyramid
- A tri-tier architectural diagram (Top, Heart, Base Notes) delineated by horizontal hairline dividers.
- Raw ingredients displayed with botanical binomial names in `body-sm` italics, accompanied by origin tags in `label-sm`.
